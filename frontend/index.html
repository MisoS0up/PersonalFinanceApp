Budget Tips

Needs: essentials such as food, housing, utilities, transport, and required bills.

Wants: optional spending such as dining out, entertainment, shopping, and subscriptions.

Savings: money for emergencies, short-term goals, and future plans.

Invest: money placed into assets for long-term growth. Only invest what you can leave invested.
